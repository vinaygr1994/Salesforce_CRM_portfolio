Real project

Case study: a home-improvement contractor in Southern California

The challenge

Leads were coming in, but site visits and results lived in texts, calendars and memory.

Reps booked in-home appointments by hand, managers chased visit outcomes, and marketing couldn't see which ads produced sales.

Industry
Home improvement & remodeling
Platform
Salesforce Sales Cloud + Experience Cloud
Scope
Design, build, test, go-live, support
Role
Salesforce admin & automation lead

What I built

01

Sales Visit automation engineA custom Site Visit object as the data spine, with Flows that create, assign and update visits and keep Lead and Visit data in sync both ways.

02

SMS + calendar automationDialpad SMS through External Services for confirmations and reminders, calendar events for in-person and second meetings, and Google Calendar sync.

03

Rep portal & secure feedback formsAn Experience Cloud portal and tokenised guest forms (secure Apex token generator with test class), so reps submit visit results from their phone.

04

Results to the team, instantlyA flow sends visit results to the team the moment a visit completes, and a scheduled flow back-fills results onto the Lead.

05

Reporting that replaced a paid toolA "Leads with Sales Visits" report type plus a Google Sheets + Looker Studio dashboard for ad spend, cost per lead and conversion.

✓ Manual visit dispatch removed✓ Duplicate records fixed at the source✓ Ad-to-sale visibility✓ Tested before go-live

Questions

Before you ask

We already have Salesforce. Can you work with what we have?

Yes. Most projects start with an existing org. I audit what's there, keep what works, and fix or rebuild what doesn't, without disrupting your team.

Do we need Field Service licences?

Not always. Many contractors run visits, jobs and agreements well on Sales Cloud with custom objects and Flows. If dispatching crews at scale needs Field Service, I'll tell you up front.

Will our team actually use it?

Adoption is designed in: simple page layouts, mobile-friendly actions, fewer clicks and short training by role. Weekly reports show who is using it and where.

Can you move our data from spreadsheets or another CRM?

Yes. Data is cleaned, de-duplicated and mapped before import, and tested in a sandbox first.

Where are you based and what hours do you work?

Process IQ Automation works with contractors in the US, Canada, the UK and Europe, with overlap on your business hours for calls and go-lives.
