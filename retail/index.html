Questions

Before you ask.

Do I need the WhatsApp Business API, or can my staff use their phones?

For automatic welcome messages, images, buttons and broadcasts you need the official API. I set it up on your business number with a verified display name. Meta charges per conversation, separately from the ad budget.

What is DLT, and do I need it for SMS?

In India, promotional and transactional SMS must use a DLT-registered sender ID and approved templates. I handle the registration and template approvals as part of setup.

How much should I spend on Meta ads?

Most single-store showrooms start between ₹30,000 and ₹1,00,000 a month, higher in wedding season. We start small, measure cost per walk-in, and scale what works.

Can it connect to my billing software?

Yes. Purchases can be imported from your billing or POS system, so the CRM knows who bought what, and Meta learns which ads drove real sales.

Who owns the customer data?

You do. The CRM, WhatsApp number and ad accounts are set up in your business name, and you can export everything at any time.
